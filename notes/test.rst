NtD fluxes for a circular boudary
=================================

The exterior Dirichlet problem:

.. math::
	\begin{cases}
	\Delta u + k^2 u = 0 & \text{in }\mathbb{R}^2\setminus{\overline{\mathrm{B}_R}}\\
	u = f & \text{on }\partial\mathrm{B}_R\\
	\partial_r u - ik u = o\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{r}}\right) & \text{as }r\to\infty
	\end{cases}

has a known solution in terms of the Fourier transform of the function :math:`f\circ\mathbf{x}` where :math:`\mathbf{x}` is a parameterization 
of the the circle of radius :math:`R`.
